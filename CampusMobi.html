CampusMobi

CampusMobi is a proposed campus assistant system designed to help university students access important academic and campus services from one platform.

Students have to carry physical student cards and rely on multiple disconnected systems to manage their academic and campus activities. This causes a lot of problems like lost cards, difficulty keeping track of schedules, and challenges navigating campus environments. These limitations create an inefficient and sometimes frustrating experience for students when trying to access services or stay organized.

CampusMobi aims to provide students with a more convenient and organised way of accessing important campus information and services.

Project Objectives

  • Provide students with easy access to campus information.
  • Provide a digital student card.
  • Help students manage their academic timetable.
  • Provide access to academic results.
  • Assist students with campus navigation.
  • Provide convenient access to registration information.

Proposed Features

  1. Digital Student Card
  2. Campus Map
  3. Student Wallet
  4. Academic Timetable
  5. Academic Results
  6. Registration Information
